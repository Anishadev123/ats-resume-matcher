About ATS Resume Matcher

ATS Resume Matcher evaluates alignment between candidate CVs and employer job descriptions using proven Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval methodologies.

Framework: Python Flask
PDF Parser: PyPDF2 (multi-page text stream reader)
NLP Pipeline: spaCy en_core_web_sm (tokenization, stopword removal, lemmatization)
Vector Model: scikit-learn TfidfVectorizer
Scoring: Cosine Similarity ($0\% - 100\%$)

All scoring is computed deterministically by the Flask application. Your files remain processed locally in memory.
